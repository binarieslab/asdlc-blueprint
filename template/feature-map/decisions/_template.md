# The decision, stated as a sentence

Status is `proposed`, `accepted` or `superseded`. A superseded record links to the record that replaces it and is never deleted.

## Context

The situation that made a decision necessary: the problem, the constraints, and the facts that matter. Link the issue or discussion where it was decided.

## Decision

What was decided, in a few sentences or a short list. Exact values, no "maybe".

## Consequences

What follows from it, the good and the costly: what changes, what is now ruled out, and what has to be done because of it. Link the specs and docs it shapes.
